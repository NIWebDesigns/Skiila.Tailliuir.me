# Skii-la’s Kitchen 💜

A little family recipe book, filled with favourite dishes, photos and memories. The website is designed to work on phones as well as computers.

## Adding or editing a recipe

1. Open [Skii-la’s Kitchen](https://skiila.tailliuir.me).
2. Tap **Owner login** and enter the owner password.
3. Choose **Add a recipe**, or open an existing recipe and tap **Edit recipe**.
4. Add the recipe name, ingredients, instructions and a photo. Then tap **Save recipe**.
5. When you have finished, open your recipe book manager and tap **Download to send to your sweet boy**.
6. Send the downloaded ZIP file to Matthew. It contains your recipes and their photos, all together.

That’s it! Matthew can unpack the ZIP file and upload the updated recipe information and photos to the family website. 💌

## A couple of helpful things to know

- Your changes are saved in the browser on the device you used. They do not automatically appear on other devices or on the live website.
- The download is a backup to send to Matthew; it does not send the file automatically.
- Recipe links open the selected recipe on the family website. Messaging apps may show a basic link preview rather than a separate photo preview for every recipe.
- The owner password is a simple front-end lock, not secure account protection. Avoid reusing a password that protects anything important.

## Website details

The website is hosted with GitHub Pages. Keep `index.html`, `recipes.json` and the recipe photo files together in the website’s main folder. To publish an update, Matthew should upload the latest recipe data and any new photos alongside `index.html`.
