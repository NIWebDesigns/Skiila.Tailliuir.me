Your recipe book 💜

Hi Skii-la! Follow these four steps each time you want to update the family recipe book. Matthew will take care of putting your changes on the website. 💕

1

Start fresh with the live website

Tap this first to fetch the latest recipes directly from the live website. Any unsent changes in this open page will be replaced. Only do this before you start editing.

2

Add recipes or make your changes

Tap to add a new recipe. To change one, close this panel, open the recipe and tap “Edit recipe”.

3

Send your website update to your sweet boy 💜

First download your update, then send the ZIP file to Matthew in your usual messages. You do not need to open the file.

4

Clear your changes and go back to the live website

After Matthew tells you the update is online, tap this button. It fetches the newly published recipes directly from the live website and clears the changes in this open page. Do not tap until Matthew confirms.
